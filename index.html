The problem

The contradiction Maldivians feel every day.

Tourism produces large foreign-currency earnings, but an ordinary person can still struggle to obtain a few hundred official dollars.

If tourism earns billions of dollars, why is official FX still difficult for Maldivians to obtain?
Where does it settle?Some tourism payments may settle outside the domestic system first.
How much is converted?USD deposited at a bank is not automatically USD for sale.
Where does it go?Imports, remittances, SOEs, debt and genuine overseas costs consume FX.

Proposed system

Make every tourism dollar visible first.

The objective is 100% visibility, not 100% forced conversion. Verified external costs can still leave transparently.

01Tourist pays

Card, platform, transfer or cash.

02Maldives first

The full payment becomes visible in regulated settlement.

03Foreign costs

Verified suppliers, debt and commissions can leave.

04Domestic MVR

Local settlement creates real MVR demand.

05National FX liquidity

Required or excess conversion supports official supply.

06Public + reserves

Travel, medicine, education, imports and any net surplus.
