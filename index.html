Maldives · Foreign Exchange · Public Policy

Tourism earns the dollars. Why can’t Maldivians get them?

The shortage is not just about how many dollars Maldives earns. It is about where tourism money settles, how much becomes official-market liquidity, where it goes, and whether legitimate users can actually obtain foreign currency.

Executive summary

Six points explain the whole argument.

01

Maldives is earning dollars.

Tourism is already a powerful FX engine.

02

A bank deposit is not automatically public FX supply.

Businesses can retain USD for genuine foreign obligations.

03

Tourism payments should become visible in Maldives first.

Then verified foreign costs can leave transparently.

04

Domestic settlement should strengthen MVR.

Inside Maldives = MVR; genuine cross-border needs = FX.

05

The parallel market survives because of availability.

A low official rate is meaningless when dollars cannot be obtained.

06

The solution is a system, not one exchange counter.

Capture → MVR economy → national FX liquidity → public access → reserves.
